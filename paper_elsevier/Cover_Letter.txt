Dear Editor,

I am pleased to submit our original research manuscript entitled "Personalized ECG Stress Detection with Subject-Specific Baseline Normalization and Bare-Metal Edge Validation" for consideration for publication in Biomedical Signal Processing and Control.

This work addresses a fundamental bottleneck in wearable affective computing and the Internet of Medical Things (IoMT): inter-individual physiological baseline heterogeneity and the risk of unencrypted telemetry. Using single-lead ECG from the benchmark WESAD cohort (N=15, 445 windows), we demonstrate that subject-specific relative baseline calibration provides a +10.79 percentage-point leap in classification accuracy under rigorous 15-fold Leave-One-Subject-Out (LOSO) cross-validation, achieving 92.13% accuracy and 0.9493 ROC-AUC. Furthermore, we demonstrate bare-metal execution on an ARM Cortex-M4 microcontroller (STM32G474RE) performing real-time Biquad filtering in 1.87 µs and validating 10,000 physical hardware-in-the-loop (HIL) telemetry packets over UART with zero CRC errors.

This manuscript is original work, has not been published previously, and is not currently under consideration for publication elsewhere. All authors have read and approved the manuscript.
