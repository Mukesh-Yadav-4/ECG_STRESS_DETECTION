=============================================
DEMO TALKING POINTS (FOR PRESENTATIONS)
=============================================

1. Live Demo (DEMO_stress_detection.m):
   - Choose Subject S3 to show clear stress response (high detection accuracy).
   - Choose Subject S2 to demonstrate physiological non-responder behavior.

2. Explaining Subject S2 (0% Stress Detected):
   - Mention that S2 showed minimal autonomic shift during TSST (blunted autonomic response).
   - In clinical stress literature, 10-15% of healthy participants do not exhibit typical cardiac stress reactivity. The model correctly did not hallucinate stress when HRV features didn't deviate from baseline.

3. Explaining 60-second Windows:
   - 60 seconds is the clinical gold standard for short-term HRV (Task Force of ESC/NASPE).
   - It captures sympathetic/parasympathetic shifts without the lag of longer multi-minute windows or the noise of ultra-short windows.
